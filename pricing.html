What’s included in the £99 fee?

No hidden extras – everything you need to understand the battery’s condition is included in the standard price.

Included as standard

  • On-site visit by an EV-trained technician within our coverage area.
  • Dedicated EV battery health test using specialist diagnostic equipment.
  • Calculation of State of Health (SoH) percentage.
  • Real-world range estimates for typical mixed driving.
  • Overall battery grade (A–E) with plain-English explanation.
  • PDF report and mobile-friendly summary delivered by email.

Not included

  • Mechanical inspection or bodywork assessment.
  • Finance, warranty, or insurance products.
  • Battery repair, replacement or software updates.
  • Travel outside our usual coverage area (may be quoted separately).

If you’re unsure whether something is included, just ask – we’d rather clarify upfront than surprise you later.

Common pricing questions

A few quick answers to situations we’re often asked about.

  • Do you charge extra if the vehicle is at a dealership rather than my home?
    No – as long as the postcode is within our normal coverage area, the price is the same whether the test is carried out at your home, workplace, a dealer forecourt or a seller’s address.
  • Can you test more than one vehicle in a single visit?
    Yes. If you have multiple EVs at the same location, it’s usually more efficient to test them in one visit. For two or more vehicles, we may be able to offer a reduced per-vehicle rate – please contact us with details.
  • Do you charge a call-out fee?
    Within our standard coverage area, there is no additional call-out fee beyond the £99 price. For locations on the edge of, or outside, our normal patch, we’ll confirm any extra cost before you book.
  • What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?
    We understand that plans change. If you need to cancel or move your appointment, please let us know as soon as possible. We’ll do our best to rearrange, and we’ll always confirm what this means for any payment you’ve already made.
  • Is there a discount for regular dealer or fleet work?
    Yes. If you’re a dealer, car supermarket or fleet operator with regular EV volume, we can structure pricing around your monthly needs. Email hello@myevreport.com with an outline of your volume and locations.
