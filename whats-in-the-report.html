What’s in your MyEVReport

Our reports are designed to be clear and easy to understand. We take technical EV battery data and turn it into a simple summary of health, range and suitability for sale or long-term ownership.

At-a-glance overview

The first page of your report includes:

  • Battery health grade (A–E)
  • Headline State of Health (SoH %)
  • Key observations and advisories

Real-world range estimates

We provide realistic range figures based on real-world UK usage rather than optimistic WLTP estimates.

  • Estimated mixed-driving range
  • Seasonal range expectations
  • Notes on range-impacting issues

Technical detail (without the jargon)

For those who want more depth, we provide a breakdown of the technical data captured from the vehicle.

  • Measured battery capacity and SoH
  • Charge cycles and usage (when available)
  • Battery temperature and balance indicators

We also include easy-to-read explanations for every technical value.

Warranty & advisory information

Many EVs have extended battery warranties. We help you understand how protected you are.

  • Summary of manufacturer battery warranty terms
  • Estimated warranty remaining
  • Relevant advisories or concerns

This gives you a sense of both current health and long-term confidence.

For buyers

A MyEVReport helps buyers compare EVs and negotiate with confidence:

  • Understand battery health vs similar models
  • Spot good-value vehicles
  • Share the report with finance or insurance providers

For sellers & dealers

Use the report to enhance your adverts and increase buyer trust:

  • Include SoH % and grade in online listings
  • Show you’ve proactively checked battery health
  • Support your valuation with independent evidence
