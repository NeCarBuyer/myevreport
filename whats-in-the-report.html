What’s in your MyEVReport

Every report is designed to be easy to read, even if you’re not technical. We turn raw battery data into a clear picture of health, range and suitability for sale.

At-a-glance overview

The first page of your report gives you a simple summary of the vehicle’s battery condition.

  • Battery health grade (A–E) based on our assessment.
  • Headline State of Health (SoH) percentage.
  • Key observations and any immediate advisories.

Real-world range estimates

Manufacturer WLTP figures can be optimistic. We provide our own range estimates based on the data we see and typical UK usage:

  • Indicative mixed-driving range on a full charge.
  • Comments on how range may vary with season and driving style.
  • Notes on any anomalies or range-limiting issues we detect.

Technical detail (without jargon)

Behind the simple grade and SoH figure, we include a breakdown of the key technical values our test has captured from the vehicle.

  • Reported energy capacity and State of Health.
  • Charge cycle and usage information where available.
  • Battery temperature and balance indicators where the car supports them.

We translate this into plain English comments so you don’t have to be an engineer to understand what it all means.

Warranty & advisory information

Many EVs come with separate battery warranties that run longer than the basic vehicle cover. Your MyEVReport includes:

  • A summary of the manufacturer’s battery warranty terms where known.
  • An estimate of how much warranty period remains based on age and mileage.
  • Any relevant advisories we believe you should be aware of.

This helps you judge not just how the battery looks today, but how protected you may be in future.

For buyers

If you’re buying a used EV, a MyEVReport helps you compare different cars and negotiate with confidence:

  • Understand how the battery has aged vs. similar models.
  • See whether the asking price fits the battery health.
  • Use the report as part of your records for finance or insurance.

For sellers & dealers

If you’re selling or stocking EVs, the report gives buyers a reason to trust your advert:

  • Include SoH % and grade in listings to stand out.
  • Show you’ve proactively checked the health of the battery.
  • Support your valuation with independent data.
