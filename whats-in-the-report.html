What’s in your MyEVReport?

Every MyEVReport is built to give you a clear, honest view of an EV’s battery – without expecting you to be an engineer. Here’s exactly what you can expect to see in the report.

The first page gives you the key information you need in seconds: an overall battery grade, headline State of Health and simple real-world range estimates.

  • Battery health grade (A–E)
  • Headline State of Health (SoH) percentage
  • Estimated real-world range in typical driving
  • Simple “traffic light” style indicators

This page is ideal for adverts and online listings – it gives buyers an instant view of the vehicle’s battery health.

The key sections of your EV battery report

Each report follows the same structure so that buyers, sellers and dealers quickly know where to look for the information they need.

1. Vehicle & test details

We start with the basics so there’s no ambiguity about which vehicle was tested and when.

  • Vehicle registration, make and model
  • Odometer reading at time of test
  • Date and location of the test
  • Ambient temperature and basic conditions

2. Battery State of Health (%)

The core of the report: how healthy the battery is compared to when the car was new.

  • Headline SoH percentage
  • Simple explanation of what this means
  • Context for the vehicle’s age and mileage

3. Real-world range estimates

Estimated range in real conditions, not just the original brochure figure.

  • Mixed driving range estimate
  • Urban / stop-start estimate
  • Typical winter range guidance

4. Battery grade (A–E)

A simple grading system to help compare vehicles quickly.

  • A = Strong battery health, suitable for retail sale
  • B–C = Expected wear for age and mileage
  • D–E = Noticeable degradation or advisory items

5. Warranty overview

A summary of the manufacturer’s battery warranty position as far as we can reasonably establish.

  • Original battery warranty term (years / mileage)
  • Estimated remaining time or mileage
  • Any important notes or caveats

6. Notes & advisories

Context and commentary where it matters, written in plain English.

  • Any unusual readings or patterns
  • Charging behaviour or thermal observations
  • Suggestions for buyers, sellers or dealers

How you can use your report

The report is designed to work hard for you – in adverts, negotiations and long after the sale.

For buyers

  • Use the battery grade and range estimates to compare multiple EVs.
  • Share the report with friends, family or a trusted mechanic.
  • Use the findings to negotiate on price where appropriate.

For sellers

  • Attach the report to your online listings or send it to interested buyers.
  • Use the SoH %, grade and range estimates as part of your advert copy.
  • Show that you’ve proactively had the battery checked.

For dealers and fleets

  • Use reports internally for stocking and pricing decisions.
  • Provide copies to retail customers as part of the handover pack.
  • Keep reports on file to track portfolio battery health over time.

What the report is – and what it isn’t

A quick clarification so expectations are clear for everyone involved in the sale.

What it is

  • An independent snapshot of the battery’s condition at the time of testing.
  • A way to understand range and degradation in practical terms.
  • A useful tool for buyers, sellers and dealers when agreeing a fair price.

What it isn’t

  • It is not a full vehicle inspection or mechanical check.
  • It is not a guarantee of future performance or reliability.
  • It does not replace the manufacturer’s own diagnostics or warranty process.
