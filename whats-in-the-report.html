What’s in your MyEVReport

Our reports are designed to be clear and easy to understand. We take technical EV battery data and turn it into a simple summary of health, real-world range and useful evidence you can share with buyers, dealers or finance.

At-a-glance overview

The first page of your report gives you a simple summary of the battery’s condition and how that translates into day-to-day use.

  • Headline battery State of Health (SoH %)
  • Clear battery status (plain English)
  • Summary of overall battery condition
  • Key observations and advisories

Real-world range estimates

We provide realistic range figures based on real-world UK usage rather than optimistic WLTP estimates.

  • Estimated mixed-driving range
  • Indicative motorway range
  • Seasonal range expectations
  • Notes on range-impacting issues

Battery warranty & history context

Your report also explains how the battery’s current health sits within the manufacturer’s warranty and typical age-and-mileage expectations.

  • Battery warranty length and remaining years / mileage (where available)
  • How the tested SoH compares with typical examples of the same model
  • Any recommendations for monitoring or further investigation

Battery status & verification (Generational+)

MyEVReport is powered by Generational+ diagnostics. Instead of an A–E rating, your report provides a clear battery status (for example: Excellent) alongside the data that supports the result.

  • Battery status – a clear outcome based on the diagnostic result
  • SoH % – the battery’s remaining capacity versus when new
  • Range guidance – practical estimates for typical UK driving
  • Assessment ID + QR verification – a certificate-style result that can be verified

The full PDF is designed to be shared alongside adverts, part-exchange appraisals or finance applications to give everyone confidence in the battery’s condition.

Each completed test includes a clear certificate-style confirmation — ideal for adverts, buyer confidence and dealer files.
