How MyEVReport works

A straightforward, mobile service designed for EV buyers, sellers and dealers. We keep things simple and transparent.

1. Book your report online

Tell us about the vehicle, your location and preferred dates. We confirm your appointment and assign an EV battery technician.

  • Enter the vehicle registration and postcode
  • Choose a date and time window
  • Pay securely online to confirm your slot

2. We visit and test the vehicle

Our technician comes to your chosen location, connects to the vehicle, and runs a dedicated EV battery health test.

  • Typically 10–20 minutes on site
  • No need for cables or chargers
  • Test can be done on a driveway or forecourt

3. Receive your MyEVReport

Within 72 hours, you receive a clear EV battery report by email, ready to share with potential buyers, dealers or finance companies.

  • PDF document and mobile-friendly summary
  • Includes our battery health rating and notes
  • Ideal for adverts and negotiations

Why an EV battery health check matters

The traction battery is the single most expensive component in a used EV. A clear picture of its health is crucial whether you’re buying, selling or stocking electric vehicles.

Protect your purchase

Avoid surprises by understanding how the battery has aged and how that affects real-world range and future value.

  • Battery replacements can cost several thousand pounds
  • Range and value are directly linked to battery condition
  • A report helps you negotiate with confidence

Increase trust when selling

A MyEVReport helps your vehicle stand out online and in person by demonstrating transparency and giving buyers confidence.

  • Ideal for private adverts and dealer listings
  • Shows you’ve proactively checked the battery
  • Helps justify your asking price

Simple, transparent pricing

One clear price for private buyers and sellers. Discounted pricing is available for dealers and fleets testing multiple EVs.
