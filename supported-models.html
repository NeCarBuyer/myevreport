Is my vehicle supported?

MyEVReport supports most modern fully electric vehicles (EVs) currently on sale in the UK. Use the checker below to confirm whether your make and model is supported for an EV battery health report.

Our testing equipment connects directly to the vehicle’s battery management system to assess battery health, State of Health (SoH) and real-world range. Because different manufacturers use different systems, not every vehicle can be tested in the same way.

What does “supported” mean?

A supported vehicle is one that we can reliably test using our diagnostic equipment to produce a full MyEVReport battery health assessment.

If your vehicle is supported, we can:

  • Read the battery’s State of Health (SoH)
  • Assess real-world range estimates
  • Produce a clear, shareable battery health report
