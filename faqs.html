Frequently asked questions

If you’re new to EVs or to battery health testing, it’s completely normal to have questions. We’ve answered the most common ones below.

About MyEVReport and the service

  • What is MyEVReport?
    MyEVReport is an independent EV battery health reporting service. We visit the vehicle, carry out a battery health test and provide a clear report showing State of Health (SoH %), real-world range estimates and a simple battery grade.
  • Are you a car dealer?
    No. We don’t buy or sell vehicles and we don’t offer finance or warranties. Our role is purely to provide independent information about the battery’s condition so that buyers, sellers and dealers can make better decisions.
  • Where do you operate?
    We currently operate across the North of England, with a focus on the North East. You can check your postcode and browse typical areas we cover here.
  • Who is the service for?
    Private buyers, private sellers, dealers and fleet operators. The process is the same, but how you use the report may be different – for example, in adverts, negotiations or stocking decisions.

The booking and test process

  • How do I book a report?
    You can request a booking online via the Book a report page. We’ll confirm coverage, compatibility and availability before sending a secure payment link.
  • What happens on the day of the test?
    An EV-trained technician visits your chosen location, confirms the vehicle details and connects specialist diagnostic equipment to the vehicle. The test usually takes 10–20 minutes. We do not need to plug into your home charger.
  • Do I need to prepare the vehicle?
    Ideally, the vehicle should have a reasonable state of charge (not almost empty) and be accessible so we can connect to the diagnostic port. You don’t need to clean the car or take it to a workshop – driveway or forecourt is fine.
  • How long does it take to receive the report?
    We aim to deliver your report within 72 hours of the test. If we miss that window for any reason, your fee will be refunded.
  • Can I be there during the test?
    Yes. You’re welcome to be present while the test is carried out. If you can’t be there, we simply need safe access to the vehicle and keys.

About the report itself

  • What exactly do you measure?
    We look at battery State of Health (SoH %), derived from manufacturer and battery-management data, and use this to estimate realistic range in everyday driving. We also apply our own grading system (A–E) and provide notes and advisories in plain English.
  • Is the report easy to understand?
    Yes. The first page is designed to be read in under a minute, with clear labels and explanations. More technical values are included further into the report for those who want the detail. You can preview the layout on the What’s in the report page.
  • Can I share the report with buyers or dealers?
    Absolutely. The report is yours to share with potential buyers, dealers, finance providers or anyone else involved in the sale. Many sellers use it directly in their online listings.
  • Is the report a guarantee or warranty?
    No. The report is a snapshot of the battery’s condition at the time of testing. It is not a warranty or a guarantee of future performance. It also does not replace the manufacturer’s own diagnostics or warranty process.

Pricing and trade questions

  • How much does a report cost?
    For private buyers and sellers, the standard price is £99 per vehicle. You can see a full breakdown on the Pricing page.
  • Is there a discount for multiple vehicles?
    Yes. If you’re a dealer or fleet operator with regular EV volume, we can structure pricing around your monthly needs. Email hello@myevreport.com with your typical volumes and locations.
  • Do you charge a call-out fee?
    Within our standard coverage area, there is no additional call-out fee beyond the agreed report price. For locations on the edge of, or outside, our usual patch, we’ll confirm any extra cost before you book.
  • What if you can’t complete the test?
    If for any reason we are unable to complete the test (for example, incompatible vehicle, access issues or equipment problems), your fee will be refunded.

Limitations and other questions

  • Is this a full vehicle inspection?
    No. We focus solely on the EV’s traction battery. We do not inspect brakes, tyres, bodywork or other mechanical components. We recommend combining our report with a trusted mechanical inspection if you want a complete view of the vehicle.
  • Can you test plug-in hybrids (PHEVs)?
    In many cases yes, but compatibility can vary. When you submit a booking request, include the full vehicle details and we’ll confirm whether we can test it.
  • Do you need to drive the vehicle?
    Typically, no. Most tests are performed with the vehicle stationary. In some cases we may recommend a short drive as part of the assessment, but this will always be agreed with you first.
  • What if I still have questions after I receive the report?
    You’re always welcome to reply to the report email with questions. We’ll do our best to explain anything that isn’t clear.
