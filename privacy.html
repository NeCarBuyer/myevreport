Privacy policy

This page explains how MyEVReport collects, uses and protects your personal information. It is a general template and may be updated as our service develops.

Who we are

MyEVReport provides independent EV battery health reports. When we say “we”, “our” or “us” in this policy, we’re referring to MyEVReport.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at hello@myevreport.com.

What information we collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • Information you provide directly – for example when you book a report or contact us.
  • Vehicle information – details such as registration, make and model, mileage and battery data.
  • Technical information – limited information from your browser or device when you visit our website.

Examples of personal data we may collect include:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number).
  • Address or location details (such as postcode).
  • Booking, appointment and communication history.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage EV battery health reports you’ve requested.
  • Communicate with you about bookings, appointments and reports.
  • Improve our services and understand demand in different areas.
  • Meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Legal basis for processing (UK / EU)

If UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases to use your data:

  • Contract – where processing is necessary to provide the service you’ve requested.
  • Legitimate interests – for example improving our service or keeping basic records.
  • Consent – where you’ve asked to receive optional updates or marketing.

How long we keep your data

We keep booking and report information for as long as reasonably necessary for our records and to answer questions about past work. We keep financial records for the period required by law.

Who we share information with

We don’t sell your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business (for example, forms, email and hosting providers).
  • Professional advisers (for example accountants) where necessary.
  • Authorities where we are required to do so by law.

Where your data is stored

Our website and online tools may store data in the UK, EU or other countries, depending on the providers we use. We aim to work with reputable providers that offer appropriate data protection safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as:

  • Accessing a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correcting inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@myevreport.com. We may need to verify your identity before we can act on your request.

Cookies and website analytics

We may use simple analytics tools or cookies to understand how the website is used and to keep it secure. Any non-essential cookies will only be used in line with current cookie regulations.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We’re not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so we recommend checking their policies if you follow a link.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our service or the law. The “Last updated” date below will always show the latest version.

This privacy policy is a general template and does not replace legal advice for your specific situation.
