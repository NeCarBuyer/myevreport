What happens after you submit the form?

We try to keep everything clear and predictable. Here’s what you can expect once you’ve requested a booking.

1. Availability check

We confirm that your postcode is within our coverage area and that the vehicle is compatible with our testing equipment.

2. Booking confirmation

We email you to confirm your appointment date and time window, and send a secure payment link for the £99 report fee (or agreed trade rate).

3. On-site visit

On the day, an EV-trained technician visits your location, carries out the battery health test and confirms when your report will be ready.

4. Report delivered

You’ll receive your MyEVReport by email within 72 hours of the test, including State of Health %, range estimates and our battery grade.
